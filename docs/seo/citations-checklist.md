## Reviews

- Ask real customers, after a completed visit, to review on Google. A printed QR code at the
  counter linking to the Google review form works well.
- Reply to every review, good or bad, politely and without customer details.
- Never write reviews yourself, pay for them, or offer gifts for them. Google removes such
  reviews and can suspend the profile.
- The website does not show star ratings in search results (no review structured data):
  Google does not allow it for reviews a business collects about itself.
